    STAY CALM

    Emergency guidance

    Simple reminders to help you take the next safe step.

    Medical emergency

    Call emergency services. Follow the dispatcher's instructions. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.

    Fire or smoke

    Move away from danger, use stairs rather than lifts, and call emergency services from a safe location.

    Personal safety

    Move to a public, well-lit place if possible. Contact emergency services and someone you trust.
